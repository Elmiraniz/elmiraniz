## 🚀 Why I Joined a Startup

After working in an academic research environment at Cornell, I wanted to experience what it takes to turn imaging research into a **real product used outside the lab**.

Joining a startup gave me the opportunity to work far beyond a narrowly defined research role.

At iSono Health, I have been able to wear many different hats — working across:

**ultrasound physics → algorithms → AI → software → hardware → clinical studies → product development → regulatory considerations**

That breadth has been one of the most valuable parts of the experience.

Startups force you to learn quickly, solve problems outside your comfort zone, understand how different parts of a technology company connect, and take ownership of problems from beginning to end.

For me, joining a startup was a deliberate decision to **grow from being a specialized researcher into someone who understands how complex medical-imaging technology is actually built, validated, and brought toward the clinic.**
